HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective

Complete the hidden pattern by clicking the correct cells in the grid. Each level has a different logical rule to discover.

🪞 Symmetry Pattern

Mirror the lit cells from left to right. Click the cells that complete the reflection.

🔢 Number Sequence

Fill in missing numbers following a pattern (Fibonacci, primes, squares). Click cells with the correct numbers.

🔲 Binary Logic (AND/OR/XOR)

Cells light up based on logical operations between rows and columns. Deduce the gate and complete the pattern.

🌈 Color Chain

Follow the color gradient sequence. Click the next color in the pattern.

➕ Row/Column Sum

Make all rows and columns sum to the same value. Click cells to balance the grid.

⚡ Tips

• Use hints wisely (1 per level)
• Complete before half time for bonus points
• Maintain combo streak for multipliers
• Watch the pattern rule description!
